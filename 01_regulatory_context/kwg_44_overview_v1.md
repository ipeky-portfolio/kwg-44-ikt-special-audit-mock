# KWG §44 – Special Audit Overview

## Legal Basis
- §44 Kreditwesengesetz (KWG)
- Authority of BaFin to conduct special audits

## Purpose of §44 Audits
BaFin uses §44 KWG audits to assess
whether institutions comply with regulatory requirements
and effectively manage material risks.

## Typical Triggers
- Material incidents
- Weak supervisory assessments
- Complex IT landscapes
- High outsourcing dependency
